# Finite experiment design

Runs are comparable only when task, dataset manifest and split, preprocessing, metric definition, evaluation protocol, and execution architecture match. Use training and validation evidence for selection. Do not use final test for search or early stopping.

Reuse the platform step and attempt during recovery. Network retry retains the same idempotency key. Select at most one next slot per Turn. Stop when the Campaign budget or slots are exhausted, the hypothesis is resolved, evidence is insufficient, results are unstable, or further improvement is not justified by the remaining bound.
